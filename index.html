How I work

A practical approach to security architecture.

I start with system context, identify credible failure paths, and work with engineering teams on controls that can be implemented and maintained.

01 / Understand context

Map the system and its trust boundaries.

Review architecture, identities, data flows, dependencies, and operational constraints before assessing risk.

02 / Assess risk

Prioritize credible attack paths.

Use threat modeling, code review, and offensive testing to connect design decisions to realistic impact.

03 / Define controls

Turn findings into engineering decisions.

Recommend specific controls, owners, and verification steps, then integrate them into design reviews and development workflows.
